Northlake Systems — Software Engineer, Cloud Storage Infrastructure
Sunnyvale, CA (Hybrid)

About the role

Northlake Systems builds the distributed storage platform behind large-scale
analytics and AI training workloads. Our storage engineering team owns the data
path: the services that place, replicate, and serve petabytes of customer data
across thousands of nodes with predictable tail latency.

We are looking for an early-career engineer to work on the control and data planes
of our block and file storage products.

What you will do

- Design and implement services in Go and Java that manage volume placement,
  replication, and failure recovery across storage clusters.
- Work close to the operating system: Linux storage stack, NVMe devices, SSD
  endurance characteristics, and kernel-level I/O behavior.
- Extend our support for network storage protocols including NFS, SMB, iSCSI, and
  NVMe-oF.
- Build observability into the data path — metrics, tracing, and tooling that make
  latency regressions obvious before customers notice them.
- Participate in design reviews for distributed systems problems: consensus,
  quorum, partition tolerance, and consistency trade-offs.
- Improve performance through profiling, benchmarking, and careful measurement
  rather than guesswork.

What we are looking for

- BS or MS in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
- Proficiency in at least one systems language: Go, C, C++, or Java.
- Solid grounding in Linux, networking fundamentals, and concurrent programming.
- Familiarity with distributed systems concepts and their failure modes.
- Experience with benchmarking, profiling, and performance analysis.
- Strong written communication; we operate through design documents.

Nice to have

- Exposure to storage protocols (NFS, SMB, iSCSI, NVMe-oF) or filesystem internals.
- Experience with Kubernetes, Terraform, or infrastructure automation.
- Contributions to open-source infrastructure projects.
- Familiarity with RAID, erasure coding, or data durability modeling.
